Al Mohaseb Al Zaky — AI Accounting SaaS

Client: UAE-based company (freelance)  |  Role: Designer & Builder  |  2026

0
AI Entries Posted Without Human Approval
2
Channels: WhatsApp & Telegram
RLS
Multi-tenant Data Isolation

Overview: A WhatsApp/Telegram-based accounting SaaS built on Node.js/Express, TypeScript and PostgreSQL. Users send invoice photos in chat; the system extracts, validates and reconciles the data, and nothing reaches the double-entry ledger until a person approves it.

Ingestion
Telegram webhook ingestion with multi-page invoice grouping, so a single invoice split across several photos is processed as one document.
Schema-Enforced Extraction
Invoice extraction on GPT-4o Vision with a strict JSON Schema and Zod validation as the final gate. A provider-abstracted extractor layer supports OpenAI, OpenRouter and a fake adapter for tests.
Deterministic Reconciliation
Extracted line items are cross-checked against invoice totals, and the stored confidence score is capped when they do not reconcile.
Human Approval & Ledger Posting
A mandatory human-in-the-loop confirmation step (web and Telegram) before any AI-extracted data is posted to the double-entry ledger, with tenant data isolated by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security.

"Zero-Inventory" AI Story Engine

Client: Vito Gifts  |  Role: Architect & Builder

Minutes
Per Order, End to End
0
Human Intervention
100%
Automated Pipeline

Overview: A fully automated "Zero-Inventory" digital product built on Make.com. The pipeline captures Salla e-commerce webhooks, generates a personalized children's story with OpenAI, creates matching illustrations with Leonardo AI, and delivers a custom PDF to the customer automatically.

Phase 1: Order Ingestion & Hero Identity Generation
A custom webhook captures Salla order payloads. An LLM parses the child's attributes and outputs strict visual prompt constraints to keep the character consistent across every illustration.
Phase 2: Story & Illustration Generation
A structured system prompt has the LLM write the story in Arabic and the scene prompts in English. A Make.com iterator sends each scene to Leonardo AI with strict negative prompting for a consistent visual style.
Phase 3: Compilation & Delivery
The final story and images are compiled into a print-ready PDF and delivered to the customer automatically, completing each order in minutes with no manual work.
Reliability: Break/Ignore error directives and timed pauses inside iteration loops respect API rate limits and prevent timeouts during high-volume periods.
