Case studies

Al Mohaseb Al Zaky, an AI accounting SaaS

UAE-based client (freelance). Designed and built in 2026 on Node.js/Express, TypeScript and PostgreSQL.

Users send invoice photos over WhatsApp or Telegram. The system extracts, validates and reconciles the data, with each tenant's records isolated by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security.

No AI-extracted entry reaches the double-entry ledger until a person approves it.

  1. Ingestion

    Telegram webhook ingestion with multi-page invoice grouping, so one invoice split across several photos is processed as one document.

  2. Schema-enforced extraction

    GPT-4o Vision under a strict JSON Schema, with Zod validation as the final gate. The extractor layer supports OpenAI, OpenRouter and a fake adapter for tests.

  3. Deterministic reconciliation

    Line items are cross-checked against invoice totals, and the stored confidence score is capped when they do not reconcile.

  4. Human approval and posting

    A mandatory confirmation step, on the web or in Telegram, before anything is posted to the ledger.

Zero-inventory AI story engine

Vito Gifts. Architect and builder, on Make.com with Salla, OpenAI and Leonardo AI.

A fully automated digital product. The pipeline captures Salla order webhooks, writes a personalized children's story, creates matching illustrations and delivers a custom PDF to the customer.

Each order completes in minutes, end to end, with no human intervention.

  1. Order ingestion and hero identity

    A custom webhook captures the Salla order. An LLM parses the child's attributes and outputs strict visual constraints that keep the character consistent across every illustration.

  2. Story and illustrations

    A structured system prompt has the LLM write the story in Arabic and the scene prompts in English. A Make.com iterator sends each scene to Leonardo AI with strict negative prompting.

  3. Compilation and delivery

    The story and images are compiled into a print-ready PDF and delivered to the customer automatically.

Reliability: Break and Ignore error directives and timed pauses inside iteration loops respect API rate limits and prevent timeouts during high-volume periods.
